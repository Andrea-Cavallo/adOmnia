# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want adOmnia to be a professional, respectful, and useful project for developers working on API, backend, integration, and enterprise workflows.

Participants are expected to communicate with respect, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussions technical, specific, and actionable.
- Welcome different levels of experience.
- Credit ideas, reports, and contributions fairly.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Insults, threats, or harassment.
- Publicly posting private information.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Discriminatory or sexualized language.
- Bad-faith issue spam or intentionally misleading reports.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

To report a conduct issue, contact the maintainer privately using the contact address listed in [.github/SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md).
