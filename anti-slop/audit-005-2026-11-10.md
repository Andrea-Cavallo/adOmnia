# First cemetery stage and reference-based visual pass

Reviewed the finite first-stage flow and current S/D/Down controls against the supplied design brief and visual references.

- Stair textures retain native proportions; gothic arches are confined to flat bridge surfaces. The former repeated stretched columns are removed.
- The wider view exposes more of the route and reduces actor scale. Ground enemies emerge from a validated surface, remain harmless while emerging and throw soil particles.
- Crouching has its own sprites, shorter hurtbox and lowered throw origin. D is no longer mapped to rightward movement; holding S/D repeats attacks at their normal cooldowns.
- A continuous panorama leads toward the chapel; the cursed tree, framed crypt interior, courtyard defence, checkpoint and Grave Guardian arena provide physical landmarks.
- Foreground gravestones and transparent mist add depth without hiding mandatory route geometry. HUD objectives and equipment remain within the narrow viewport.
- The first stage has a real completion screen after destroying the bell and reaching the unlocked exit. Checkpoint restart is distinguished from restarting the stage.

Automated gameplay, type/build and browser verification are recorded in `frontend/src/assets/hub-game/STAGE1.md`. No outstanding presentation finding is recorded in this scoped pass. Manual Wails acceptance, pacing, full armor-break animation and later-stage/meta progression remain explicitly open in that document.
