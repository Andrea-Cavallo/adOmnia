# adOmnia v0.9.73 — An arcade in the Hub

Developers need a break too. Click the adOmnia wordmark in the Hub and one of
four dot-matrix mini games opens in the same box, chosen at random and never
the same twice in a row.

## The games

- **Snake** — eat, grow, speed up. Now with an accent flash when it eats, a
  fading tail and a blinking snake on game over.
- **Tris** — tic-tac-toe against an AI that plays well but sometimes slips, so
  you can beat it. Arrows and Enter, or click a square. Your wins are counted.
- **Pong** — you on the left, the AI on the right, first to 5. The ball speeds
  up on every return.
- **Asteroids** — turn, thrust and fire; big rocks split into small ones, the
  screen wraps and every cleared field brings a bigger wave.

## One look, one set of keys

All four games are drawn on the same dot matrix, in the spirit of a Nothing
Phone display: the text color for pieces, the accent color of your palette for
highlights, score and best score in a dot-matrix font. They work in Dark and
White and with any palette.

- Arrows or WASD to move, Space or Enter as the action key
- P to pause, Enter to play again after a game over
- Esc, or the Esc button, returns to the wordmark
- The best score of each game is kept on your machine

## Fixed

- **Arrow keys no longer reach the app while you play.** The game owns them
  only while it is active: click outside the box and it pauses, handing the
  keyboard back to adOmnia.
