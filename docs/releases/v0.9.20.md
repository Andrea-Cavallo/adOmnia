# v0.9.20 — a0 finds its platforming rhythm

- Progressive ground acceleration, responsive braking and separate air control. Running now reaches 330 px/s.
- Hold Space for a full jump; release early for a short hop. Coyote time, jump buffering and double jump remain available.
- **C: ground slide. C → Space: long jump.** The slide lowers a0 and carries momentum into the jump; it does not grant invulnerability. X/Shift retains the separate dash.
- Holding Space on a stomp gives a high rebound; releasing it keeps the bounce low. Stomps restore the air jump and dash, enabling chains without firing.
- Developer Desk now connects stepped books, ground bugs, an armoured stomp and a flying stepping stone to a rewarded upper ledge. The safe lower route remains available.
- Gremlin and Phantom are optional roaming encounters. Only Legacy Brute closes its expanded arena; its slam physically leaps and its charge travels across the arena, followed by a recovery window.
- Common enemies have distinct silhouettes. Removed the detached-looking wrist weapon; weapon projectiles, recoil and ammo HUD remain.
- English/Italian controls explain the slide and held-jump/rebound mechanics. Local records and accessibility preferences remain offline.
