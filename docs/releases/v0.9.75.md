# adOmnia v0.9.75 — The Hub fits on one page

The Hub is back to a single screen: no scrolling to reach what matters.

## A lighter Today column

- The workspace card, the **Open workspace** button and the
  **Today's requests** counter are gone from the Hub. The workspace stays one
  click away in the rail and the status bar; full request history stays in
  **History** and in **Recent activity**.
- **Start something new** — Open project, New request, Add connection — moves
  up right under the dot-matrix clock and the Arcade.

## Tighter layout

Studio cards and page spacing are slightly more compact, so the whole Hub,
including Recent activity, fits without scrolling from 1280×760 upwards, in
Dark and White and with any accent palette.
