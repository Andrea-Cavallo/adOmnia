# adOmnia v0.9.72 — FOLD, a wordmark and a game of Snake

The Hub's Today column gets a living centerpiece: FOLD, a folded-metal object
that greets you with the adOmnia wordmark made of particles, and hides a
dot-matrix Snake behind it.

## FOLD

- **Wordmark first:** when the Hub opens, a cloud of dots gathers into the
  adOmnia wordmark, which sways gently and leans toward the pointer. It takes
  the accent color of your palette, in Dark and in White.
- **Six forms:** left alone for five minutes, the wordmark slowly dissolves
  into FOLD's six folded-metal forms (idle, listening, thinking, searching,
  tool call, answer), which change every few seconds with soft transitions.
  Click the forms to bring the wordmark back. The metal always stays white.
- **Knows when you are waiting:** while a request is in flight, the forms hold
  the "thinking" shape.
- **Light on resources:** Three.js is loaded only when the Hub is open and is
  not part of the app's startup. With reduced motion enabled, FOLD shows a
  still frame.

FOLD is based on the open-source motion study by Ship Notes (MIT).

## Snake

Click the wordmark and the box turns into a Snake board on a dot matrix, in
the spirit of a Nothing Phone display:

- the snake in the text color, food in the accent color;
- score and best score in a dot-matrix font, best kept on your machine;
- arrows or WASD to play, Space to pause, Enter to retry after a game over;
- Esc, or the Esc button, returns to the wordmark.

The snake speeds up as it grows.
