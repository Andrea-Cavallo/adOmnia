# v0.9.19 — Bug Hunt enters the developer world

The three-stage campaign now builds toward the Legacy Monolith: unstable desk platforms, moving production infrastructure, then timed rule overrides. a0 keeps its reactive personality and exactly three lives for the entire run.

- **Buggy Dev Desk:** keyboard keys, IDE windows, books, phones and USB platforms; a collapsing keyboard bridge; 21 enemies, including flying bugs, Timeout drones, zombies and a five-hit NullPointer guarding the exit.
- **Production Datacenter:** rack lifts, fan columns, data packets and cycling pods. Memory Leak grows, Race Condition telegraphs its charge, and Deadlock fires in both directions. CPU pressure briefly slows the environment while movement stays responsive; an overheating wave forces an escape.
- **Legacy Dimension:** Windows-style dialogs, XML, floppy disks and CRT backgrounds grow visually corrupted. Approach warnings introduce reverse gravity, offline platforms and summoned clones before the arena.
- **Legacy Monolith:** six health segments and three phases: SOAP Storm, Legacy Override and Monolith Panic Mode. SOAP/XML/500 volleys become more intense; the final phase drops warned Java 6 debris and requires destroying CORE, SESSION and SOAP before the final hit.
- **Controllable gravity:** the boss announces GRAVITY_OVERRIDE with a three-second countdown. Inversion lasts five seconds, the ceiling supports movement and jumping, and the green Gravity Safe Zone restores normal gravity beside the boss. Leaving the arena cannot freeze the command timer.

## Local storage compatibility

Records use `adomnia.bughunt.preferences.developer-world.v5`, because the encounters and scoring changed. Audio and reduced-motion settings migrate from the first existing v4/v3/v2/v1 profile. Earlier records and keys remain untouched. No workspace format change, telemetry or cloud service.
