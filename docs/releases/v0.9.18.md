# adOmnia 0.9.18 - a0 has a personality, Localhost is twice the level, and the campaign has three lives

## a0 reacts

a0 is no longer a mask that slides across the screen. The eyes track the nearest enemy or projectile inside a threat radius, the eyebrows and mouth tense as that threat closes in, and nothing about the hitbox changes — the tell is all in the face.

Landings squash proportionally to the impact speed and spring back. Standing on the edge of a slab makes a0 wobble for balance. Reaching a checkpoint raises both arms. With gentle effects enabled the poses stay, but the oscillation and deformation are removed.

Contextual speech bubbles read in IT and EN — "Sul mio PC funzionava" after a death, "Salvato. Respiriamo" at a checkpoint, "Era tutto calcolato" after a close call — and are positioned above the popups so the two never overlap.

## Localhost is a full level

Localhost grows from 3220 px to 4820 px, from 8 enemies to 15, with extra platforms and grapple anchors and 21 additional bits (156 across the campaign). The Hotfix moved to x=4590 and the exit to x=4700. API Gateway and Production keep their original geometry and exits.

## Three lives, one campaign

Three hearts sit at the top of the HUD and cover the entire campaign. A hit or a fall costs one; checkpoints and environment changes do not refill them. At zero the simulation stops and offers a campaign restart or an exit. A defeat never writes a victory record.

Records live in `adomnia.bughunt.preferences.three-lives.v4`, which migrates only the audio and reduced-motion preferences from the first existing key among `arcade.v3`, `campaign.v2` and `v1`. The old keys are left untouched, and the `.adomnia` workspace format is unchanged.

## Verification

`tsc --noEmit` clean, `npm run build` clean, **560 frontend tests across 113 files** (58 Bug Hunt tests), `go build ./...` and `go test ./...` passing. The game tests cover three consecutive losses, restart, lives carried between environments, the full extended Localhost route, expressions and poses, and the preferences migration.

The menu, the hearts and the new poses were reviewed on screen in a real browser using a temporary scene, removed after the check. A full human desktop playthrough of the three-life campaign was still open when this release was cut because `wails3` was not installed in that environment.
