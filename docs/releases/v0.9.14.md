# adOmnia 0.9.14 - Bug Hunt: keep the chain going

Bug Hunt gains short combat sequences and optional scoring challenges, built on the three-stage campaign and developer power-ups.

- Hit a bug to recharge the dash immediately. Link a stomp, an air dash and another jump through the optional aerial encounters.
- Every environment offers a Bonus Rush: collect six bits in eight seconds for 500 points. Missing the bonus costs no health and does not block the exit.
- The final screen awards a score rank from C to S and shows completed rushes.
- Boss damage earns points once per health milestone, even after retries. The Legacy Monolith warns before its final double wave.
- Fixed canvas sizing that could crop the bottom of the level; hints now sit above the route instead of covering a0.
- Existing controls, developer powers, reduced motion and offline storage remain available.
