# adOmnia 0.9.15 - Bug Hunt: the floor is being deleted

Bug Hunt gains a chase sequence and a movement power that turns the campaign into a chain of aerial moves.

- **The floor is being deleted.** In Production a `DELETE` wave wakes up behind a0 and eats the level from the left. Platforms it passes stop being solid and leave only a dashed outline. Outrunning it to the checkpoint pays 400 points; stopping costs a life and resets the chase. Four new high ramps offer the fast line, and the Breakpoint power freezes the wave along with the rest of the world.
- **Magnetic grapple.** Press `E` to latch onto the nearest glowing node above a0, within 300 px. The cable is a real pendulum: radial speed is cancelled, tangential speed is kept, so a0 swings. `W` and `S` reel the cable in and out; a shorter cable swings faster. `Space` or releasing `E` launches a0 and refills the dash, so grapple → launch → dash → bug chains without touching the ground.
- Anchors sit above every main-route gap in all three environments, nine of them in Production. They are always optional: the ground route stays open and a missed latch costs nothing.
- English and Italian copy, HUD pills, key legend and stage hints cover both mechanics.
