# adOmnia 0.9.13 - Bug Hunt becomes a campaign with developer powers

The hidden hub game grows from a single level into a three-stage campaign with
a boss, new moves and four developer power-ups. Nothing else in adOmnia
changes; the game still loads only when opened and runs fully offline.

## A three-stage campaign

Spin the hub logo three full turns, or hold it for 30 seconds, to open it.

- **Localhost:** the original level, now with springs and reward trails.
- **API Gateway:** moving platforms that carry a0, firewalls that warn before
  they switch on, and Retry bugs that keep hopping.
- **Production:** platforms that crumble under your feet, a checkpoint before
  the arena, and the **Legacy Monolith** boss: dodge its ground wave, then land
  on the exposed core three times.
- Stage-clear screens carry time and bits across the campaign; the final screen
  shows score, time, bits, deaths and best combo, with local records.

## New moves

- **Double jump:** press Space again in the air.
- **Dash (X or Shift):** a short burst that defeats bugs and crosses firewalls,
  with a visible recharge.
- **Combos:** chaining bits and bugs raises a score multiplier up to x5, without
  rewarding farming.

## Developer power-ups

Each stage hides four chips, readable by glyph and label:

- **↺ git revert** - collect charges (up to three) and press **R** to rewind the
  last three seconds of movement. A missed jump or a bad landing becomes a
  second try. Falls clear the history, so you never rewind into a pit.
- **⏸ Breakpoint** - the world pauses for five seconds: bugs, firewalls, moving
  platforms and the boss freeze, marked with a breakpoint dot, while a0 keeps
  moving.
- **# sudo** - six seconds of root access. Bugs are deleted on contact and
  spikes, firewalls and boss waves do no damage. The aura blinks before it ends.
- **♻ Garbage Collector** - a shockwave sweeps the screen and frees every bug it
  reaches, printing `free(0x…)` as memory is reclaimed.

The HUD shows revert charges and active timers, the key legend includes R, and
every line is available in English and Italian.
