# adOmnia v0.9.76 — Your colors, a smarter Tris

## Personal appearance

- **Settings > Appearance** now picks a **primary** and a **base** color.
  Preview them on a card or across the whole app before applying; Cancel
  restores the saved style.
- Save up to **50 named profiles** locally and switch between them.
  Surfaces, text and buttons derive readable shades automatically.
- New installs start from a monochrome look: black with white in Dark,
  white with black in White. Existing accents and custom themes are kept.

## Base color from the status bar

Right-click **Dark** or **White** in the status bar: below the accent color
you can now pick that mode's base color. Each mode remembers its own base,
so clicking Dark or White no longer resets it to the adOmnia default.

## Arcade

- **Tris** plays perfectly: it wins as soon as it can, always blocks your
  threats and never makes random moves. A draw is a good result: 3 points
  per win, 1 per draw.
- The arcade keeps the three best games: **Snake, Tris and Pong**.
  Asteroids, Dot Bubble and Glyph Racer are gone.

Everything stays local: no accounts, no telemetry, no cloud.
