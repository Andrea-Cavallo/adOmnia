# adOmnia 0.9.16 - Parallel WebSocket sends, and a boss that rewrites the rules

## WebSocket: parallel send

The WebSocket client can now fire a burst instead of one frame at a time.

- Separate commands with a line containing only `---`. Each command is sent `N` times concurrently, with `N` set next to the **Send parallel** button (1–500).
- `{{$i}}` resolves to the 1-based index of each send, so a burst can carry distinct payloads.
- The result lands in the session log as `ok/total` with the elapsed milliseconds, and the first error when something fails.
- Ordinary sends, variable resolution and the payload modes are unchanged.

## Bug Hunt

The hidden game gets its combat layer, and the Legacy Monolith becomes a real final boss.

**You can shoot back.** Hold `F` to fire debug packets: 640 px/s, a short cooldown, light recoil. They close bugs, knock **SOAP envelopes out of the air**, and armoured bugs take two.

**Bugs react.** Two new kinds join the patrols:

- **Chasers** wake when a0 comes within 320 px, shout, and charge at 196 px/s — without ever leaving their band of solid floor.
- **Turrets** hold a post, track a0 within 430 px and return fire every 1.85 s, with a dashed aim line and three charge pips so every shot is announced before it can hurt.

**The Legacy Monolith rewrites one rule per phase.** It announces the command for 1.7 seconds — banner, HUD pill and sound — and only then executes it:

| Phase | Command | The arena becomes |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| 3 | `sudo reverse gravity` | Up is down. a0 falls upward onto the arena ceiling and has to jump **down** onto the core. |
| 2 | `fork() → 3 clones` | Three clones spawn and charge. Shoot them, or let them run past. |
| 1 | `systemctl stop platforms` | The arena slabs drop one at a time. Standing still stops being an option. |

Each rule is local to the arena, ends the moment its phase does, and the Breakpoint power-up freezes it along with everything else.

**The Monolith itself** is now a tower: a CRT face whose eyes track a0 and change shape when the core opens, the stack it refuses to retire (`JAVA 6`, `EJB`, `WSDL`, `JAX-WS`, `STRUTS`, `ORACLE`), two sticky notes nobody dares remove, and two fists on cables that lob **SOAP envelopes** in an arc across the arena. Its old ground wave is gone.

**The secret is easier to find.** Spinning the home-screen logo one full turn now asks whether you want to play, instead of opening the game after three turns or a 30-second hold. The 30-second wait is gone, and the invite can be dismissed. `README.md` now admits the secret exists without saying how it works.

## Verification

`tsc --noEmit` clean, `npm run build` clean, **552 frontend tests across 113 files** (50 game tests), `go build ./...` and `go test ./...` passing, `npm audit` reporting no vulnerabilities.

The boss arena was also inspected on screen, in a real browser: the announcement banner, the SOAP volley, the Monolith at rest and a0 hanging upside down from the ceiling under reversed gravity. That pass caught four layout defects — a duplicated announcement over the boss's face, the state line printed across the legacy stack, wall graffiti on top of the checkpoint terminal, and a0's `aO` reading as `90` when flipped — all fixed before release.

Difficulty and feel still need human playtesting.
